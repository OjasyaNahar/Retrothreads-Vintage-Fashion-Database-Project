RetroThreads
Fashion Heritage Management System

RetroThreads is a digital platform designed to preserve and document vintage fashion heritage while enabling authenticated buying and selling of vintage garments. The system combines cultural archiving with a controlled marketplace to ensure that historically significant fashion pieces are preserved, studied, and responsibly circulated.

The platform stores detailed garment records including physical attributes, designer information, fashion era, and stylistic characteristics. Each garment is linked with provenance data such as runway appearances, museum displays, or media associations, along with supporting authenticity documents.

RetroThreads also supports a verified marketplace where collectors, sellers, and institutions can list and transact authenticated vintage garments. Role-based access ensures that only approved users can add, edit, or sell items, maintaining data integrity and trust.

The system provides search and filtering capabilities that allow users to explore garments by designer, era, style attributes, provenance, price, and condition. This makes the platform useful for research, education, and general exploration of fashion history.

Target users include fashion museums and galleries, vintage collectors and sellers, fashion historians and researchers, design students and educators, and general fashion enthusiasts.
